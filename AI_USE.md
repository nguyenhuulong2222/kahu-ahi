# Use of AI

This project's code, its documentation and the Kahu Ahi mark were made with an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic, in chat and through Claude Code), working to the team's requirements and under the team's review.

- **Code and documentation.** Written with the AI assistant and reviewed and run by the team. All project code was written after the hackathon started (14 Nov 2026, 9:00 AM HST). Before the hackathon, AI was used only to study the challenge statement, the participant rules and NASA FIRMS documentation and to plan the approach; no project code from before the hackathon is in this repository.
- **Numbers.** Every figure in the README and on the page comes from running the pipeline on NASA FIRMS data, not from the AI. An independent recomputation and its findings are in [AUDIT.md](AUDIT.md); that audit was also carried out with the AI assistant.
- **Images.** The Kahu Ahi mark (`docs/kahu-ahi-mark.svg`) and the link-preview image made from it (`docs/kahu-ahi-og.png`) were made with the AI assistant, as above. They carry no visible "AI-generated" watermark. No photograph or video in this project was generated by AI; any added later will be listed here and carry a visible "AI-generated" watermark.
- **Demo video.** The narration of the 30-second demo (`demo/kahu-ahi-demo.mp4`, voice track `demo/narration.wav`) is an AI-generated voice (Google Gemini), supplied by the team; the end card says "Narration: AI-generated voice" and the file's `comment` tag says the same. The picture is a screen recording of the live page. Its recording script, captions and editing were made with the AI assistant. It contains no AI-generated images or video; the only drawn element is the Kahu Ahi mark described above, on the title and end cards.
